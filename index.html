Business Idioms

Picture flashcards for business English

Learn the idioms you hear at work

180 business idioms, one clear picture each, and three example sentences from easy to hard. Nine themes: meetings and presentations, communication, projects and deadlines, finance and money, sales and customers, HR and careers, workplace life, decisions and problem solving, strategy and growth.

  • Flip through cards and switch between easy, medium and hard sentences.
  • Type each sentence from memory with word-by-word feedback. An idiom counts as learned once all three sentences are typed, and your review pile is one tap away.
  • Works fully offline. No account, no ads, no tracking.
